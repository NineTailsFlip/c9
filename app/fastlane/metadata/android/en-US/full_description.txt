Calendars and Contacts on 9 keys. A calendar and address book for phones with a physical keypad
and no touchscreen. It reads and writes straight to Android's own calendar and contacts, so any
sync adapter (CalDAV/CardDAV, Google, ...) keeps working underneath.

Features:

* Day, 3-Day, Week and Agenda views, paged with the d-pad
* Create and edit events, including repeats, attendees, reminders and color
* Reminder notifications with Snooze and Dismiss
* Search events, and choose which calendars are shown
* Contacts grouped alphabetically, with T9 search by name or number
* Create, edit and delete contacts; manage groups, favorites and accounts
* Favorites can sync through a CardDAV group
* An in-app manual covering every key

No internet permission, no ads, no analytics, no third-party libraries. Syncing is left to the
sync apps you already use.

Part of Nine, a suite of apps for keypad phones: https://nine.furrer.life
